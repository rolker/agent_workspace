**Serves**: Quality of the work (agents learn from what is there; a safeguard earns its cost) and Trust, control and information (the user's limited attention: the skill's pacing, one-liner-first and question-count rules) — closest design section `docs/design.md#instruction-layers` (layer 3, skills).
**Design picture?** No change to how the parts fit: a new skill adds a `SKILL.md`, not a new mechanism. The skill count in `docs/design.md#instruction-layers` ("22 skills in `.claude/skills/`") goes stale; `ls -d .claude/skills/*/` shows 23 directories today, so that line needs a recount when the skill lands. If the second mode writes into the roadmap or the design document (comment M1/D1), that touches the design and goes to the user on its own.
**Dependencies**: none identified as blocking. Related: #335 (design work it grew from, closed by #377/#380/#382), #398 (orchestrator design session, may want the same conversation rules).
